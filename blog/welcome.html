Welcome to the Porch Project Blog

A little about why we started this, what you'll find here, and how to get the good stuff delivered straight to your inbox.

Steiner Ranch is full of good people, great local businesses, and more going on than any one of us can keep track of. The Porch Project started as a simple idea: gather it all in one trusted place, built by neighbors, for neighbors.

This blog is the next chapter. Alongside our business directory and summer camp planner, it's where we'll share the stories behind the storefronts and the people who make this community what it is.

What you'll find here

Expect a mix of the practical and the personal:

  • Local spotlights — meet the makers, owners, and neighbors behind your favorite spots.
  • Neighborhood guides — the best of Steiner Ranch, season by season.
  • Community news — events, openings, and the stuff worth knowing about.
Built by neighbors, for neighbors — that's the whole idea.

Get it in your inbox

The easiest way to keep up is our newsletter. Every new post and local find goes out to subscribers first — no scrolling, no algorithm, just the good stuff delivered to you. Sign up below and you're in.
