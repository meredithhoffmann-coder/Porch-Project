At Home with Family

Protect Your Tots!

From Trash to Treasure: A Tater Tot Story for Your Holiday Table

Neighbor Leslie "King Tot" Grigg is a descendant of the inventors of the Tater Tot, and he's shared a free storytelling activity for families. Pull up the picture together at the table and take turns with three questions:

  1. What could you create from something people usually throw away?
  2. When have you turned a setback into something good?
  3. What family story would you like your children or grandchildren to remember?
Who it's for
Kids, parents and grandparents together. Free, no registration.
Open the activity →

Courtesy of Protect Your Tots!™, strengthening families and preventing child abuse.
