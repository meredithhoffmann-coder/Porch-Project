Happier at Home – Austin provides compassionate, non-medical in-home care that helps seniors and adults live safely, comfortably, and independently in their own homes. We support families with personalized assistance including companionship, help with daily activities, meal preparation, light housekeeping, transportation, and respite care.

What makes us unique is our deeply personal, relationship-driven approach. As a locally owned and operated business serving Steiner Ranch and surrounding communities, we take the time to thoughtfully match caregivers with clients based on needs, personality, and preferences. We focus not just on tasks, but on dignity, consistency, and peace of mind for both clients and their families.

Customers can expect reliable, flexible care plans, clear communication, and hands-on involvement from our local leadership. We are licensed by the State of Texas and operate with a strong commitment to professionalism, safety, and compassion. Whether families need a few hours of support or ongoing care, our goal is to make aging in place feel safer, easier, and more connected.
