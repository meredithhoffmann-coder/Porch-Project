Nurturing Young Minds, One Smile at a Time Empower Kidz Drop-In is a boutique drop-in child care center and play space designed for today’s families. Located in Lakeway, TX, we offer flexible, hourly child care that makes it easy to drop off your child and step away with confidence. Whether you need care for a quick errand, a work meeting, or an evening out, your child will be supported in a warm, engaging environment built just for them. Our drop-in model gives parents freedom without sacrificing quality. Children are welcomed into thoughtfully designed indoor and outdoor play spaces where learning happens naturally through play, movement, creativity, and social connection.

As seen on KXAN, Empower Kidz Drop-In is proud to be recognized for providing a trusted, flexible child care solution that supports both children and today's busy families.
