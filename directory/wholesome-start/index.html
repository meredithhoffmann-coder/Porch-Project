Wholesome Start Nutrition Counseling provides personalized, evidence-based nutrition counseling for adults navigating digestive health concerns and their relationship with food. Founded by Samina Qureshi, RDN, LD, CSDH, a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist and Board Certified Specialist in Digestive Health with 12+ years of experience, Wholesome Start specializes in IBS, SIBO/IMO, GERD, gastroparesis, constipation, bloating, and other complex GI conditions.

Our approach is non-diet, weight-inclusive, and rooted in evidence-based nutrition. Samina helps her clients understand their individual symptoms, identify meaningful food and lifestyle patterns, and develop practical nutrition strategies that fit real life.

Clients can expect compassionate, personalized guidance without judgment, fear-based food rules, or shame. Samina has been featured as a nutrition expert on television and in numerous media outlets including KHOU and EatingWell.

Wholesome Start offers convenient Telehealth nutrition counseling throughout Texas, helping clients feel better, eat with confidence, and get back to enjoying life.
