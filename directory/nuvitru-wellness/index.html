Nuvitru Wellness is a functional and integrative medicine clinic in Austin, TX offering root-cause health programs, memberships, functional lab testing, and in-clinic therapies. Our structured programs - including Gut Restoration, Metabolic Reset, and more - are designed to address the underlying drivers of chronic symptoms, not just mask them.

Patients can expect to feel genuinely heard, thoroughly evaluated, and supported throughout their health journey. No rushed appointments, no generic advice - just personalized care from practitioners who know your case inside and out.

Our team brings specialized training in functional and integrative medicine with expertise in complex lab interpretation, metabolic health, and holistic wellness strategies. We exist for the people who've been told "everything looks normal" - and know that it isn't.
