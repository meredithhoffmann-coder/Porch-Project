They’re never just pets… they’re family.

I’ve realized in the past few months how much joy I found in painting animals. Not just because they’re beautiful, but because they’re family. They hold memories, comfort, personalities, routines — they become such a huge part of our lives.

I’ve shared my life with two cocker spaniels—Chanel and Diesel. Chanel was by my side for nearly 15 years before I lost her to cancer two years ago. There’s no way to fully describe that kind of loss… it leaves a quiet space in your heart that never quite fills again. Diesel is now 16 and still going strong—my sweet old soul, a daily reminder of how precious time really is.

Every portrait I create is more than just a painting. It’s a way to honor their spirit, their personality, and the bond that never fades, including memorial pieces that honor the ones we’ve lost but will never stop loving.

Because love like that can’t be replaced… but it can be remembered, forever. 🐾

If you’d like me to create something special for your furry family member, my commissions are now open. DM me for details.

Sizes: Mini- 5 x7 in Small- 8 x 10 in Medium- 12 x 16 in Large- 16 x 20 in All I would require are a few of your favorite photos (good quality, not blurred) of your pet, especially ones here the eyes are clear. I use watercolors on high quality premium Arches cold pressed water color paper. Each portrait is designed to keep the focus completely on your pet’s face, expression, and personality. You’ll be able to choose your preferred backdrop color, and I’ll also help you select a color that complements your pet’s fur and makes their features and eyes stand out beautifully. My portraits primarily focus on the face. Depending on the size of the portrait and the composition, I may also include part of the neck, shoulders, and collar. I intentionally do not paint the full body or detailed backgrounds. I believe a pet’s portrait should speak volumes through their expression alone — without anything in the background competing for attention. The result is a timeless, soulful portrait where your pet is the entire focus.

Each portrait takes me approximately 10-15 working days. This may vary depending on the breed, the size of the portraits selected and my availability.
