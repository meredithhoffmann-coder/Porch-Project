Joshua Tree Experts provides professional tree care and plant health services throughout Greater West Austin. Our services include tree pruning, removals, storm cleanup, oak wilt prevention, fertilization, insect and disease treatments, tree injections, and ongoing plant health care programs.

What makes us unique is our proactive, approach to protecting Austin’s trees. We do more than remove problems. We help homeowners and businesses identify risks early, improve tree health, and preserve the beauty, safety, and value of their landscapes.

Customers can expect clear communication, honest recommendations, professional crews, careful cleanup, and high-quality work from start to finish. We understand that trees are a major investment and an important part of Austin’s character, so we treat every property with care and respect.

Joshua Tree Experts is led by an ISA Certified Arborist and Certified Tree Safety Professionals and backed by a team trained in modern arboriculture, plant health care, oak wilt prevention, and safe tree care practices.
