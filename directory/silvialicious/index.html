Silvialicious

Artfully designed, scratch-made desserts

Porch Neighbor
Food & DiningBakeries / Desserts

Silvialicious is an award-winning boutique bakery in Austin, Texas, specializing in custom cookies, luxury cakes, and elegant French macarons. Founded by third-generation Italian baker Silvia Zolfanelli, Silvialicious offers scratch-made desserts crafted with premium ingredients, natural flavors, and the option for dye-free, naturally colored designs. From beautifully detailed custom sugar cookies and gravity-defying celebration cakes to corporate logo cookies and seasonal macaron collections, every creation is thoughtfully designed to be both stunning and delicious.

Silvia is also known for hosting engaging cookie decorating classes for kids, teens, and adults, creating memorable experiences that bring the community together.

What makes Silvialicious unique is its commitment to artistry without compromise—combining old-world Italian baking traditions with modern design and a focus on natural color options. Customers can expect exceptional attention to detail, personalized service, and desserts that taste as incredible as they look.

Silvialicious is proud to be a Best of West Austin award winner two years in a row (2025 and 2026), earning first place for Best Locally Made Product and second place for Best Bakery in 2026.
