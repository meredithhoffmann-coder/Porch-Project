Hearts & Arts Foundation is an Austin-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit creating interactive art and cultural experiences that bring people together through creativity, connection, and community.

We offer artist-led workshops, youth and adult creative experiences, community art events, and special programming designed to make art something people actively experience, not simply observe. Our programs also support local artists and creative entrepreneurs through opportunities to teach, showcase their work, and receive fair compensation for their talents.

What makes Hearts & Arts especially meaningful is our commitment to accessibility. Through our Creative Access initiative, we provide at least 1 sponsored seat at every event for individuals and families who may otherwise face barriers to participating in creative experiences.

Participants can expect thoughtfully curated, welcoming, hands-on experiences led by talented local artists, with quality materials and opportunities to learn, create, and connect.
