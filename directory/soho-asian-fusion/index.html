SOHO Asian Fusion 江南小屋 unites the refined culinary traditions of Jiangnan with the breadth of modern Asian cuisine in Austin. Rooted in the warmth and understated elegance of Jiangnan, our concept brings the spirit of Family dining into a contemporary, thoughtfully curated experience. Our kitchen focuses on premium ingredients, balance, and technique — offering a menu that seamlessly integrates Chinese classics, Japanese influences, and modern Asian fusion. SOHO is more than a place to dine — it is a space to gather, share, and experience flavors that are both familiar and refined.
