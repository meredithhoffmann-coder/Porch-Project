Leslie “King Tot” Grigg is an entrepreneur, keynote speaker, business consultant, and member of the family behind one of America’s most iconic food innovations—Tater Tots®.

Drawing on more than 45 years of experience in financial services, investing, sales, leadership, and entrepreneurship, Leslie helps individuals and organizations recognize overlooked opportunities, build stronger relationships, and turn ideas into measurable action. His signature philosophy is: “Bite off more than you can chew—then chew it with confidence.”

Today, Leslie is co-founder and CEO of the F. Nephi & Golden Grigg Legacy Foundation, founder of Protect Your Tots™, and producer of The Tater Tot and the Quest for the Holey Board. Through these initiatives, he is preserving the entrepreneurial legacy of his great-uncles, F. Nephi and Golden Grigg, while using that remarkable American story to strengthen families and communities.

Protect Your Tots™ focuses on practical, evidence-based parenting education designed to reduce parental burnout, improve child behavior, and strengthen families.

Leslie’s work brings together entrepreneurship, storytelling, philanthropy, and an unforgettable family legacy under a simple belief: Food Creates Connections. Stories Create Change.
