Jo’s Pest & Lawn is a locally owned pest control company serving homeowners across Austin. We provide a full range of pest services including general pest control for insects and bugs, mosquito treatments, flea and tick control, rodent control (rats and mice), wildlife management, and termite services.

What makes Jo’s Pest & Lawn different is our personalized approach. Rather than placing customers into a one-size-fits-all program, we work with each homeowner or property manager to develop solutions tailored to their specific property and pest issues. Our goal is to provide effective pest control services without that are safe for your Family and pets.

Customers can expect reliable scheduling, responsive communication, and thorough treatments from a team with decades of combined pest control experience. As a local Austin business (and a former Steiner Ranch Stallion), we take pride in providing a top notch service to this community!
