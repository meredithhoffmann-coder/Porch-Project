Art Chakra Studio, led by Austin visual artist Rashmi Thakur, is a vibrant center for learning and creativity. Specializing in art workshops, private lessons, and summer camps, all instructed by Rashmi herself, the studio nurtures artists of all ages, including kids and adults. Students explore watercolor, acrylics, oils, and textile art, with programs ranging from art basics to advanced arts and holistic art, all while receiving personalized guidance in a supportive and inspiring environment.

With over two decades of teaching experience, Rashmi mentors students and fosters artistic growth across Austin. She has been invited to teach workshops and demos at prominent Austin galleries including ArtUsCo, BCAF, Austin Palette Club, Waterloo Watercolor Group, and Dripping Springs, to name a few. Her sessions are engaging, skill-building, and designed to spark creativity, reflection, and confidence in every participant.

Beyond teaching, you can acquire original artwork or commission personalized pieces including portraits of people and animals in watercolors, acrylics, oils, and more. Rashmi has earned recognition as Austin Business Woman’s “Artist of the Year” in 2025, along with numerous merit awards in watercolor from the Waterloo Watercolor Group. Her work has been exhibited internationally in Italy and locally at Austin Bergstrom International Airport, SXSW, and the Austin Studio Tour. As President of the Waterloo Watercolor Group, she combines professional expertise with community leadership, making Art Chakra Studio a hub for creativity, learning, and artistic connection.
