Topsy Stitches is a boutique embroidery studio specializing in high-quality custom monogramming and personalized gifts. I primarily embroider customer-supplied items - everything from baby gifts and graduation towels to custom wedding linens and business logos on a wide variety of items. I also offer a curated selection of unique gifts, including wreath sashes, wedding bouquet wraps, prom and homecoming accessories, tote bags, and seasonal keepsakes.

What sets me apart is my white-glove level of service. I work closely with each client to select the perfect font, scale, and placement, providing digital proofs throughout the process. This ensures every piece feels intentional, balanced, and refined. My expert-level precision, thoughtful design approach, and elevated aesthetic are tailored to the Steiner Ranch community.

Most orders are completed within a few days, often faster with prompt proof approvals, compared to the extended turnaround times typical of storefront shops. Clients can expect responsive communication, meticulous stitching using professional-grade equipment and premium threads, and polished, heirloom-quality results for gifts, events, or everyday pieces.
