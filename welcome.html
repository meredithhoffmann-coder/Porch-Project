Settling in

Your first few weeks here

A short, unfussy list to make the Ranch feel like yours faster.

  1. Get on the newsletter

    It's how you'll hear about events, openings, and neighborhood news before it's old news. Sign up here.

  2. Line up your essentials from the directory

    Find a lawn crew, a handyman, a vet, a dentist — all recommended by people who live right here. Browse the directory.

  3. Put the next event on your calendar

    Showing up once is the fastest way to go from new-in-town to knowing faces. See what's coming up.

  4. Say hello

    Wave on your walk, introduce yourself at the mailbox, and check the events for the next Newcomers welcome — the easiest way to meet folks all at once. Small hellos add up.

    Plug in on these Facebook groups

    • Moms & Tots
    • Steiner Parents of Teens
    • Steiner Ranch Dudes
    • Steiner Ranch Social
    • Steiner Job Networking Group
    • Buy & Sell Steiner
    • Everything Free Steiner Ranch

  5. Handle the practical stuff

    Set up utilities and find your trash and recycling days, then get familiar with the HOA basics on the Steiner Ranch HOA site — it's also where you'll book the tennis and pickleball courts and reserve community spaces. Ask in the newsletter reply if you're stuck — a neighbor will know.
